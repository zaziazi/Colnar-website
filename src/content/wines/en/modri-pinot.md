An elegant Modri pinot that gains extra depth, complexity and refined character over 12 months of
ageing in oak barriques.

The aromas weave together notes of ripe cherries, wild strawberries and gentle spices, rounded out by
subtle touches of vanilla and oak. On the palate the wine is silky, with soft tannins, a balanced
structure and a long, elegant finish.

The harmonious union of fruit and wood brings out the varietal elegance of the Modri pinot and gives it
a character of its own.
