Our house white is a fresh line of wine, equal parts kraljevina, laški rizling and sauvignon. After
fermentation we age and keep it in stainless steel until bottling. It is dry, with a pale yellow
colour.

It carries the freshness and liveliness the three grapes bring. The nose is full and floral, with
subtle notes of citrus and tropical fruit. On the palate, fresh flavours develop with a light mineral
edge that sharpens its elegance. Dry and pleasantly acidic, it is at its best as an aperitif, or with
fresh salads, fish or poultry.
