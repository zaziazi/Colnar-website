Cviček is the speciality of Dolenjska, and it accounts for a full 60 % of the wine we make. You could
call it a sprightly young man: pronounced freshness, at home with red meat and cured sausage. What
makes cviček unusual is that it is blended from both white and red grapes. It is large in its
smallness and unique in its make-up, and in it you find the softness of the Dolenjska hills and the
cheerful nature of the people who farm them. The blend is žametna črnina, kraljevina, laški rizling
and modra frankinja.
